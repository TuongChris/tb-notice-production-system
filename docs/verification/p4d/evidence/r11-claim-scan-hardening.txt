The weak pattern
----------------
A scan for forbidden wording over the textContent of a DOM subtree that holds several elements. textContent
joins the text of adjacent elements with nothing between them, so wording a reader sees can escape a scan:
  - a word-boundary pattern misses a word followed by the next element's text ("G1 PASS" + "Selection" reads
    "G1 PASSSelection"; "Send" + "notice" reads "Sendnotice");
  - a phrase across two elements without whitespace escapes a phrase pattern ("Ready to" + "sign" reads
    "Ready tosign");
  - whitespace on both sides of a node boundary spaces a phrase twice ("Ready for " + " signer").
The P4D negative control NC-P4D-18 found it in the P4D page scans; 1b012bd replaced them by a walk over the
page's text nodes joined by a space. That helper has two gaps of its own: it replaced the textContent reading
instead of adding to it (a word split across nodes, "G" + "1" + " PASS", is whole only in textContent) and it
did not collapse whitespace (React renders {'Ready for '}{'signer'} as two text nodes of one element, which it
read "Ready for  signer" — control HC-08 below: the accepted P4D scans missed it).

The hardened reading (tests/web/support.tsx: claimTexts(root = the page))
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Three views, and a forbidden pattern must match none: (1) textContent as is — exactly the reading every earlier
scan used, so no scan becomes weaker; (2) the same with whitespace runs collapsed; (3) the text nodes joined by
single spaces, whitespace collapsed. Each hardened assertion keeps its pattern and its subtree; only the
reading widens: `expect(pageText()).not.toMatch(P)` became `for (const text of claimTexts()) expect(text).not.toMatch(P)`.
tests/web/claim-texts.test.tsx (new, 1 test) pins the reading: textContent misses the first three cases above
and finds the split word; claimTexts finds all four; its first view is textContent itself.
